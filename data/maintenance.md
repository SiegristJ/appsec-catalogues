# Maintenance procedure


Every change follows the same steps, so the catalogue, its appendices and the audit register stay consistent. Roles, approval rules and review cadence are set in the audit register’s Governance section.

**Triggers.** An area’s watch list fires; the quarterly watch-list check finds a new edition or dataset; the annual full review; a critical disclosure; a review finding or error report.

**Steps.**

1. Verify the new evidence first-hand and record each source as an R-ID in Appendix B, with its tier and whether it was opened.
2. For a changed fact, update its row in Appendix A, then every location in that row’s “Used in” column.
3. Edit the affected areas: threats, controls, residual risk, “Facts and sources”, and the “Last reviewed” date.
4. Regenerate the derived sections: applicability index, baseline and conditional controls, residual-risk summary, compliance mapping, and the “Used in” and “Cited by” columns of Appendices A and B. Never hand-edit them.
5. Add or update claims-register rows and write the change-log entry.
6. Obtain approval, plus specialist review if controls or residual risk changed.
7. Run the consistency check defined in the audit register.
8. Release: date and number the version and list it in the change log.

**ID rules.** IDs are never renumbered or reused. A retired item stays in place marked “Retired”; a replaced item is marked “Superseded by” its successor. Each threat has one home area; other areas cross-reference it.

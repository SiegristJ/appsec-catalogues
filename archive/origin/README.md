# Origin archive — frozen

**Frozen. Superseded. Not authoritative.**

This directory holds the material the catalogue's version 1 was built from, kept
read-only as a historical record:

- `source-reports/` — the two AI deep-research reports the work started from
  (the Claude report; the Gemini report, archived in both its docx and Markdown
  exports, which are identical content from the same source file).
- `reconciliation/` — the cross-report reconciliation that compared them and
  settled their differences.

Nothing in the catalogue depends on this material as evidence. It should never be
cited, quoted or used as a source. Why it is kept, and what it may and may not be
used for, is stated in [`HISTORY.md`](../../HISTORY.md) under "Status of the source
reports and reconciliation". Questions about current content go to the catalogue and
register; questions about how version 1 was assembled come here.
